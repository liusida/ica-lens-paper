Non-Gaussian directions isolate entity attributes. Cause vs. Isolation averaged over six attribute test sets (city: country, continent, language; Nobel laureate: birth country, field, gender). SAEBench RAVEL on Gemma 2 2B (residual stream, layer 12), default configuration (top 500 entities, 5000 pairs per attribute, 2 epochs, mean of 2 seeds; whiskers show the range). Cause: fraction of interventions that switch the queried attribute to the source entity's value. Isolation: fraction of interventions that leave the entity's other attributes unchanged. Grey lines mark equal Disentangle = (Cause + Isolation) / 2. ICA, PCA and Random use sign-split coordinates of an exactly invertible basis; SAE is Gemma Scope 16k (average_l0_41).
