Interpreting language models without training another dictionary

The hook

The same token can carry different internal stories.

In the paper view, the bank example summarizes the top ICA components for financial-bank and river-bank occurrences. In the explorer, readers can click through tokens, components, signs, labels, scores, and examples.

ICA and SAE

Related lenses, different shapes.

SAE features often look like localized events. ICA directions often trace broader contextual factors across neighboring tokens. The point is not replacement; it is a cheaper first pass before deciding where an SAE is needed.

Evidence across models

Not just a few nice examples.

Across GPT-2 Small, Gemma 2 2B, and Qwen 3.5 2B Base, ICA recovers compact non-Gaussian directions that are inspectable and useful under sparse probing and targeted intervention.
